# Frozen-controller physical delay sweep

Prospective protocol, 8 October 2026. This follows the
[collective-suppression experiment](../results/collective_suppression/README.md)
committed at `d9f7e9f`. Its purpose is to isolate the effect of command latency
within the same learned controller, plant and disturbance condition. It does
not retrain networks or test how suppression emerges during training.

## Fixed controller and variable temporal embedding

Use all 48 validation-selected transformers from the original 4×4 plant/noise
grid, three initialization seeds per cell. Keep each model on its own original
plant tau and force-noise correlation time. Weights, plant damping, feature
scales, history length, action bound and force-noise amplitude remain unchanged.
Keep observation and decision intervals at 50 ms. Sweep physical command
latency through **0, 25, 50, 75, 100, 150 and 200 ms**. Hold commands between
applications. The simulation clock, independent 2.5 ms force clock and physical
plant propagation are inherited unchanged.

The encoder has an explicit planned-delay feature. In the primary experiment,
replace only the policy-facing planned application time with dispatch time plus
50 ms before encoding. The simulator still schedules command application using
the actual swept latency. This holds the announced-delay input at its training
value and avoids simultaneously manipulating a direct cue to the network.
Observation histories, already-applied actions and their ages remain truthful;
they can change because the feedback loop itself changes. This is an intentional
latency perturbation without an updated delay cue, not a controller optimized
or retrained for every deployment delay. A truthful-delay-cue arm is a separate
future experiment.

At latencies longer than 50 ms, several stateless snapshot queries are pending
at once. This is a prescribed latency experiment with idealized parallel
throughput, not a serial processor slowing its decision cadence. Preserve the
existing event convention: at a common timestamp, capture precedes application
generated by that timestamp's completion event; subsequent dispatch sees the
newly applied action. Do not alter the historical simulator to change this
convention. Record and check job spacing and application latency.

## Frozen pathways and controls

Use the exact groups selected by the prior common-history discovery and the
exact five variants saved in its `dynamics_prepared` records:

- Native model.
- Jointly weakened group (branch scales 0.9).
- Jointly strengthened group (branch scales 1.1).
- Output-gain control calibrated to the weakening effect at 50 ms.
- Alternative-group control calibrated at 50 ms.

Freeze branch membership, gate scales, output gain, centering constant and
command offset across all delays. Do not recalibrate, recenter or select a
different group at any delay. Verify the prior record's model identity,
checkpoint hash, discovery dependency and settings. Missing controls remain
explicitly unavailable; never substitute a newly selected control.

The gain and alternative controls were calibrated at the original 50 ms
condition, with magnitude/direction matching success recorded. That eligibility defines a fixed model
cohort for control comparisons at every delay. Their effective matching can
drift on changed histories. On fresh native histories at every delay, measure
immediate command-change RMS, pulse-response direction and mean shift for the
frozen variants. These diagnostics describe match drift; they do not refit
controls or select a new confirmation cohort. Preserve failed baseline matches.

## Fresh paired confirmation

Use force-noise seeds **3010001–3010004**, checked to be disjoint from inherited
training, validation, discovery, calibration and confirmation streams. Pair the
same exogenous noise tape across physical delays, variants and pulse/sham arms.
At a given plant/noise condition, models share environmental tapes. Each model
still has only three independently initialized training seeds per timing cell.

Use four-second episodes from zero state. Pulses begin at 1 s, last 0.1 s and
have amplitudes −0.02 and +0.02. Each seed/variant/delay has one unique sham and
two pulse trials. Score over [1,4] s using the inherited physical metrics. The
force standard deviation remains 0.02. Native input histories needed for the
matching diagnostics are recorded from these already-required native rollouts,
not generated through extra physical simulations. Encode those recorded
histories with the same fixed delay cue as the controller.

Run a passive zero-command policy once per plant/noise cell on the same four
tapes and pulse signs, shared across model seeds and delays. This provides an
absolute competence comparator. Its commands are identically zero, so physical
command latency does not affect its trajectories. Do not use passive outcomes
to select models or modify the delay grid.

Expected physical simulation count, if all inherited variants are available:
48 models × 7 delays × 5 variants × 4 tapes × 3 arms = **20,160 own-loop trials**,
plus 16 plant/noise cells × 4 tapes × 3 arms = **192 passive trials**. Total:
**20,352**, with no new training, discovery or calibration. Count repeated shams
once; exclude development pilots and the independent integration smoke test.

## Primary causal contrast and secondary outcomes

Let E_v(d) be mean paired pulse-recovery energy for variant v at delay d,
averaged equally across four tapes and two signs. Each energy is the integral
of squared pulse-minus-own-sham position difference divided by squared pulse
amplitude. This is a finite-horizon incremental response, not a complete task
utility or a proof of stability.

Define the within-model weakening effect Δ_w(d) = E_weak(d) − E_native(d).
The primary interaction is **Δ_w(100 ms) − Δ_w(0 ms)**. A positive interaction
means weakening becomes more harmful, or less helpful, as latency increases
over this declared contrast. Also report the strengthening interaction. The
interaction alone does not establish a sign reversal: separately count models
with weakening helpful at zero delay and harmful at 100 ms, and the opposite
pattern. Report model-level paired contrasts, not differences between cohorts.

Alongside absolute differences in this recovery metric report percentage effects
100 × [E_v(d)/E_native(d) − 1] and their high-minus-low percentage-point
interaction. Show absolute native and modified energies so changing native
denominators cannot be mistaken for changing intervention strength. Ratios
require native magnitude above the configured 1e-12 denominator floor.
The fixed-history command-response floor remains the inherited 1e-4 value.

Show the complete delay curves; no monotonicity is assumed and the primary
endpoint pair is not selected from observed outcomes. Other delays and metrics
are descriptive secondary outcomes. Noise-only position RMS, command RMS,
means, saturation, native/passive competence and task failures accompany
recovery. Compare baseline-calibrated controls against weakening on identical
model cohorts with the inherited eligibility labels.

Task failures that finish the horizon retain their numeric outcomes and are
flagged separately. Incomplete/censored trials have undefined required metrics.
An aggregate requiring a missing numeric outcome is undefined, not the mean
of surviving tapes or models. Keep valid/required denominators and failure
counts visible. A sign switch among resolvable outcomes is not a survival-
conditioned claim of benefit for the full cohort. No success-conditioned
reselection or baseline-competence exclusion is allowed.

## Verification, provenance and reporting

Verify the committed collective manifest and all explicitly listed source and
artifact hashes, then its inherited original model/data lineage. Copy the base
configuration unchanged for checkpoint loading. Fingerprint new scientific
source, this protocol and configuration before confirmation, and commit them
before launching the full experiment. Reporting and plotting can develop
separately without changing the frozen computation.

Tests must verify fixed-delay-cue invariance on identical histories, equality
to the original policy at 50 ms, recorded-feature/output agreement, zero gate
change identity, and true fixed-cadence application at zero, fractional and
multiple-period delays. Verify against an independently integrated fractional-
delay classical feedback example, in addition to inherited sampled-system
tests. Provenance tests must reject changed sources, unsealed dependencies,
invalid grids and overlapping probe streams. A full-pipeline smoke run uses
separate development streams and cannot be pooled into confirmation.

Publish model-level outcomes, paired interactions, seed ranges, failed matches,
failures and censoring, plotted without interpolating across undefined values.
The principal visualization is a delay-effect curve for each fixed controller,
grouped by plant/noise condition, with zero effect and the 50 ms training delay
marked. Preserve both raw recovery energy and relative effects. Report timing
checks and physical simulation accounting, and verify output hashes before
publication.

A within-controller reversal would support timing-dependent causal usefulness
of the existing suppressive pathways. It would not establish that inhibition
developed because of timing, that biological E/I balance exists, that these
branches selectively suppress high frequencies, or that an explicit E/I
architecture improves performance. Damping, closed-loop modes and delay margin
require their own measurements. Prior models were trained by expert imitation;
this follow-up uses fresh disturbance realizations but is not an independent
training replication.
